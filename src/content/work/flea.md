    'Supervoid had the honor of creating the visuals for Flea’s first-ever solo headlining tour, an intimate international run supporting his debut jazz album, Honora. For the Red Hot Chili Peppers bassist and the Honora Band, using Notch VFX we built a glowing world of amorphous spheres that drifted, pulsed, and bloomed in step with the music. The orbs were driven by both the lighting console and live input from the band, so every night’s show was its own. Martin Phillips’ dramatic lighting and minimalist production design gave the orbs room to breathe, and cinematic video directed and shot by Flea’s daughter, Clara Balzary, added a personal touch. Supervoid also provided media servers and programming for the tour.',
